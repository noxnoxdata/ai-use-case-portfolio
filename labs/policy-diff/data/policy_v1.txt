1. Purpose. This policy sets the minimum requirements for using AI tools in the bank.
2. Scope. It applies to all staff, contractors, and third-party vendors.
3. Approved tools. Staff may use only AI tools approved by the AI Governance Committee.
4. Data. Confidential data must not be entered into external AI tools.
5. Human review. Outputs used in customer decisions must be reviewed by a qualified human.
6. Monitoring. High-risk AI use cases must have a monitoring plan and a named owner.
7. Incidents. AI incidents must be reported to the AI Governance Committee within 24 hours.
8. Exceptions. Exceptions to this policy must be approved in writing by the Chief Risk Officer.
9. Review. This policy is reviewed every twelve months.
