1. Purpose. This policy sets the minimum requirements for using AI tools across the group.
2. Scope. It applies to all staff, contractors, and third-party vendors.
3. Approved tools. Staff may use only AI tools approved by the AI Governance Committee, and must re-confirm approval annually.
4. Data. Confidential data must not be entered into external AI tools. Personal data may be processed only in approved environments.
5. Human review. Outputs used in customer decisions must be reviewed by a qualified human, and the review must be recorded.
6. Monitoring. High-risk AI use cases must have a monitoring plan, a named owner, and a quarterly review.
7. Incidents. AI incidents must be reported to the AI Governance Committee within 24 hours.
8. Training. All staff must complete annual AI awareness training.
9. Review. This policy is reviewed every twelve months.
